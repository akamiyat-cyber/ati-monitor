3F4D Site Viewer

Graphs of the 3F4D Water Monitor sheets of several sites

Getting started - do the steps in order

Steps 0-3 are done once for each site. Steps 4-5 are done in this viewer.

Steps 0-3: prepare the Google Sheet of each site
0
Watch the video manual (4.5 min)

Shows steps 1 to 5 on the screen. Optional, but recommended the first time.

Download the video (MP4, 10 MB)
1
Download the sheet template

One file for each site. Choose the language of the sheet.

For practice: demo sheets of 3 sites with data (do steps 2-5 with them):
2
Make the Google Sheet and enter the settings
  1. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it.
  2. Choose File > Save as Google Sheets.
  3. Fill in the yellow cells of the tab 1_Settings (site name, sowing date, seedling age, plot area, TP and b).
  4. Daily data are entered later in the tab 2_Daily_input.
3
Share the sheet and copy the link
  1. Click Share (top right of the sheet).
  2. General access: Anyone with the link, role: Viewer.
  3. Click Copy link (or the site sends you this link).
4
Paste the link here and press "Add site"

Repeat for every site. Nothing is written to the sheets.

5
See the graphs

The summary and the graphs appear below. Press the button to read the newest data of all sites.
