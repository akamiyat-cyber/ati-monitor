Help

3F4D manual

How to practise 3F4D+MD (needs internet):

1. First time

  1. Setup tab: enter ATI name, place, sowing date, seedling age, and TP / b of both plots (items marked * are required).
  2. Schedule tab: planned dates appear automatically. Enter the real date in "Actual" when each event happens.
  3. Share tab > Shared Google Sheet: type the shared sheet link and the compare link given by your teacher.

2. Every day

How to read m, TP and b:

Tap the figure to enlarge. m = reading at the water surface, TP = reading at the top of the pipe, b = height of the pipe top above the ground. Water level = m - TP + b.
  1. Entry tab: choose the date, enter the tape reading (m) of FP and 3F4D+MD. If you irrigated, answer Yes and enter the readings before and after irrigation.
  2. "Save entry" is enabled only when all required items (*) are filled and the values are in range.
  3. Press Share > Copy my data, open the shared sheet, click the first empty cell of column A in the tab "Data" and paste.

3. Correct a mistake

  • Water-level entry: in the Entries table press ✎, change the values and press Save entry again (same date = overwrite). ✕ deletes the day.
  • Fertilizer, pesticide, tillers, TP/b: press ✎ in the list, change, press Update. ✕ deletes.
  • After any correction, press "Copy my data" and paste again below the last row. The newest paste of your ATI is used.

4. Fertilizer, pesticide, tillers

Entry tab > Field records. Tillers: count 5 hills; the value used is the mean of the 2nd, 3rd and 4th largest.

5. Compare

Compare tab > "Load from shared sheet". Select the ATIs to show. Charts: cumulative irrigation and water level (FP and 3F4D+MD), total irrigation and saving, tiller number. To see how Compare looks without internet, use the demo (Setup > Try with demo data).

6. Demo and backup

Setup > "Try with demo data" opens example data; nothing is saved. Share > Export file (JSON) makes a backup of your data. Data are stored only in this browser.

7. Install on a phone

Open the app address in Chrome once with internet, then choose "Add to Home screen". After that it also opens without internet; paste your data into the sheet when you are online.

Shared Google Sheet

The shared sheet is made by your teacher. No password or script is needed: you copy your numbers here and paste them into the sheet.

  1. Press "Copy my data".
  2. Open the shared sheet (button below), go to the tab "Data" and click the first empty cell of column A (below the last row).
  3. Paste (Ctrl+V, or long-press and Paste on a phone).

Share a view-only link

The link contains a snapshot of all data (nothing is uploaded to a server). Anyone who opens it sees the dashboard, read-only. After you add new entries, create a new link.

Backup and restore

Data are stored in this browser only. Export a file regularly as a backup, or to move the data to another phone.

About

ATI Water Monitor works offline after the first visit. Schedule rules follow the 3F4D+MD scheduling app (Professional mode).
